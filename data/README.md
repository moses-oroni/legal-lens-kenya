# Pilot dataset

`kenya_digital_law_case_pilot_v0.1.csv` contains 10 purposively selected Kenyan superior-court decisions coded for an exploratory Legal Lens study.

## Coverage

- Decisions: 10
- Decision dates: 2014–2026
- Courts: High Court, Court of Appeal, Supreme Court
- Primary source: Kenya Law
- Verification cut-off: 26 August 2026

## Important cautions

- The sample is not comprehensive or statistically representative.
- Each summary is a compressed paraphrase; read the linked judgment before relying on it.
- Appellate and implementation status must be rechecked.
- `lens_observation` contains provisional researcher analysis, not a judicial finding.

See the [selection protocol](../docs/methodology/Case_Selection_and_Verification_Protocol.md) and [codebook](../docs/methodology/Kenya_Case_Coding_Codebook.md).
