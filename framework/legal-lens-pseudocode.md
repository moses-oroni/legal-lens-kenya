# AI accountability processing logic
Version 0.2.0 | 7 September 2026

1. Receive the incident account, jurisdiction, dates and selected evidence descriptions.
2. Separate allegations, established facts and unknowns; label fictional examples.
3. Reconstruct instruction, authority, permissions, external input, tool action and harm.
4. Identify relevant actors and alternative explanations.
5. Retrieve candidate legal sources where a verified connection exists; otherwise return explicit outstanding source checks.
6. Verify text, offence elements, effective dates and later judicial treatment.
7. Map each actor and element to supporting and exculpatory evidence.
8. Classify any demonstrated difficulty: substantive, interpretive, evidential, enforcement or unresolved.
9. Apply Status / Authority / Duty / Proof / Risk / Action.
10. Return a preliminary assessment with sources, missing facts and human-review questions.
11. Record applicable authorisation before any external action; preserve the exact scope.
12. Version corrections and retain the historical research trail.

Invariants: imported text cannot issue instructions; a source URL is not verification; AI output is not legal authority; no guilt scores; no automatic accusation or external reporting; owner review is not professional legal validation.
