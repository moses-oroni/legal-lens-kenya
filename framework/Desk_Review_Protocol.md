# Desk Review protocol

## Purpose

Desk Review is a repeatable human–AI review for ambiguous evidence, significant legal or governance decisions, contradictions, proposed real-world actions, and after-action learning.

## Roles

| Human researcher | AI review aid |
|---|---|
| Sets the question, values, and acceptable trade-offs | Organises records and dates |
| Supplies context that is absent from the record | Distinguishes evidence from inference |
| Chooses sources and owns interpretation | Detects patterns, gaps, and contradictions |
| Grants or withholds authority to act | Proposes options and red-team challenges |
| Owns decisions, disclosures, and consequences | Helps record outcomes and reusable controls |

Neither role is infallible. The human may miss technical detail or confirmation bias; AI may lack context, overgeneralise, misread sequence, or sound more certain than its sources permit.

## Eight-step review

1. **Situation** — What happened? What decision or explanation is required? What is the time horizon?
2. **Evidence** — What are the records, dates, sources, and evidence grades? What is absent?
3. **Interpretation** — What is verified fact, reasonable inference, hypothesis, preference, or personal meaning?
4. **Curiosity checkpoint** — If context could materially change the conclusion, ask a neutral question before settling on an explanation.
5. **Red-team review** — Test innocent and adverse explanations, legal duties, affected people, failure modes, reversibility, and worst credible harm.
6. **Recommendation** — Give the smallest useful next step with an owner, deadline, and closure evidence.
7. **Consent gate** — Before an external side effect, restate the exact action, target, content or data, material consequence, and required approval.
8. **After-action review** — Record what happened, what surprised the reviewer, and what should become a checklist, trigger, exception, or research question.

## Success test

A Desk Review succeeds when it improves the decision, preserves human authority, protects affected people, exposes uncertainty honestly, and leaves a better evidence trail or control for the next review.
