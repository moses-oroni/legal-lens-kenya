# Legal Lens — AI accountability framework
Version 0.2.0 | 7 September 2026

| Stage | Question | Required output |
|---|---|---|
| Status | What happened, when and where? | Allegation/fact distinction; incident timeline; AI role; missing facts. |
| Authority | Which current legal rules govern? | Exact provision or paragraph; effective date; later court treatment; verification status. |
| Duty | What must be established against each actor? | Conduct, mental element, authorisation, causation and participation questions; separate criminal/civil/regulatory routes. |
| Proof | What supports or contradicts each element? | Instructions, access records, tool calls, approvals, system version, custody and exculpatory material. |
| Risk | What could make the assessment wrong? | Compromise, injection, excessive inference, missing records, stale law and rights impacts. |
| Action | What review step is justified? | Evidence preservation, a specific source check, qualified review or a bounded authorised action. |

## Actor distinctions
Identify the prompter, deployer/operator, organisation, developer/provider and possible third-party attacker only where relevant. Do not infer criminal responsibility solely from ownership, job title, model output or an authenticated account.

## Evidence states
A: directly checked authoritative evidence for a specific proposition and date.  
B: corroborated evidence, with the corroboration identified.  
C: supplied but unverified.  
D: historical context.  
Q: conflicting material requiring resolution.

A primary-source URL without reading its relevant text does not establish A or B. A fictional example is never a verified event.

## Output boundary
The output is a preliminary incident and legal-issue assessment. It must identify uncertainty and alternative explanations. Do not declare guilt, invent duties, treat negligence as a universal criminal standard, or give numerical liability scores.

## Core research test
Ask whether the difficulty is substantive law, interpretation, evidence, enforcement or unresolved facts. Test existing-law coverage before recommending reform.


## Citation verification update — 9 September 2026
Every legal claim must pass authority existence, passage support and jurisdictional/factual applicability checks. Current legal status, adverse authority, provenance and named review are also required. A supplied URL is a lead, not verification. Failed, missing, stale or conflicting checks block verified labels and final legal approval. Corrections invalidate dependent checks and approvals. Administrative permission is not legal validation.

The controlling standard is [Citation Verification Standard](https://github.com/moses-oroni/legal-lens-kenya/blob/main/docs/methodology/Citation_Verification_Standard.md). The Alpha remains a guided checklist without live source verification; implementation status is recorded separately.
