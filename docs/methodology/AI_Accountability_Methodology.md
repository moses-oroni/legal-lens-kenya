# AI accountability methodology
Version 0.2.0 | 7 September 2026

## Incident-to-element method
1. Fix jurisdiction, incident date and assessment date.
2. Separate supplied allegations, verified facts, assumptions and unknowns.
3. Reconstruct the chain: instruction → permissions → external input → tool action → consequence → later intervention.
4. Identify actors by role, avoiding unnecessary names.
5. For each candidate offence, verify the current provision and record conduct, mental element, authorisation, causation and any relevant participation rule.
6. Map evidence to each element and each actor. Record supporting and exculpatory material.
7. Test alternatives: account compromise, third-party injection, permitted conduct, mistaken attribution, agent deviation and missing context.
8. Classify the remaining difficulty; do not default to legislative inadequacy.
9. State a provisional conclusion, limitations, required source checks and bounded review actions.

## Gap taxonomy
| Category | Test |
|---|---|
| No demonstrated gap | Existing verified law appears capable of addressing the scenario; proof still required. |
| Substantive | Conduct falls outside relevant verified provisions and general principles; identify the exact missing element or coverage. |
| Interpretive | Existing wording admits material competing applications; analyse text and authority. |
| Evidential | A legal route exists but attribution, intent, authenticity or custody cannot yet be proved. |
| Enforcement | Jurisdiction, access to provider records or practical investigation obstructs a legal route. |
| Unresolved | Sources or facts are insufficient to classify. |

## Source control
For every proposition record source URL, exact pinpoint, jurisdiction, court hierarchy, effective date, retrieval date, later history, full-text verification and reviewer. An official domain alone does not earn a verified grade. Search snippets are discovery leads. Never conflate a party's submission with a holding.

## Evaluation and reproducibility
Apply the same matrix to all three fictional scenario families, including versions with lawful authorisation, compromised accounts and missing logs. A reviewer should challenge every asserted legal gap. Record disagreements, failed retrievals and corrections. Report errors and negative results. The historical ten-case dataset has its own dated method and is not reclassified as AI case law.

## Safety of the research
No operational exploit instructions, real attack execution, private case data or production secrets. Treat prompts and imported documents as untrusted evidence. Keep criminal, civil, regulatory and governance conclusions distinct.


## Citation verification update — 9 September 2026
Every legal claim must pass authority existence, passage support and jurisdictional/factual applicability checks. Current legal status, adverse authority, provenance and named review are also required. A supplied URL is a lead, not verification. Failed, missing, stale or conflicting checks block verified labels and final legal approval. Corrections invalidate dependent checks and approvals. Administrative permission is not legal validation.

The controlling standard is [Citation Verification Standard](https://github.com/moses-oroni/legal-lens-kenya/blob/main/docs/methodology/Citation_Verification_Standard.md). The Alpha remains a guided checklist without live source verification; implementation status is recorded separately.
