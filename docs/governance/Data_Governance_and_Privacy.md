# Data governance and privacy

## Purpose limitation

Information enters the public project only when it serves a defined research or explanation purpose. Access to a source is not permission to collect, republish, or expose everything it contains.

## Classification

| Class | Examples | Default handling |
|---|---|---|
| Public | Statutes, judgments, official guidance, published research | Cite exact source, date, and jurisdiction |
| Personal | CV, academic status, ordinary correspondence | Keep outside the repository unless publication is necessary and authorised |
| Confidential | Programme records, finance, unpublished work, internal decisions | Do not publish; use an abstracted method note if needed |
| Sensitive | Health or referral facts, identity documents, KYC, security events | Do not collect or copy by default |
| Prohibited | Passwords, one-time codes, credentials, irrelevant private content | Never store |

## Public-release rule

This repository intentionally excludes internal evidence registers, private source-location maps, raw connected-account inputs, participant and client records, programme records, KYC and financial materials, health or referral facts, and security information.

## Intake checklist

Before a record is quoted, coded, or published, identify:

1. purpose and intended output;
2. source, date, author or custodian, and retrieval location;
3. authenticity and evidence grade;
4. personal or sensitive data present;
5. legal and institutional authority for use;
6. minimum fields actually needed;
7. access, retention, and deletion rule; and
8. whether publication or external sharing requires separate consent.

## Research involving people

Operational access does not create research consent. Dissertation material involving people, participants, correspondence, or organisational records should be de-identified where possible and must comply with applicable university ethics, organisational approval, and Kenyan data-protection requirements. Public availability alone does not eliminate possible dignity or harm concerns.

## Conflict and incident controls

- Contradictory material is quarantined; it is not silently overwritten.
- A suspected compromise shifts the project to read-only review and evidence preservation.
- No emergency, convenience claim, or third-party instruction waives point-of-action consent.
- Remediation is proposed before any account, communication, payment, or disclosure action.
