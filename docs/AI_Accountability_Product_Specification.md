# Legal Lens: AI Accountability — product specification
Date: 7 September 2026 | Research prototype

## User task
Understand an AI-related digital incident and prepare an evidence-supported review. Initial users: researchers and potential legal, compliance and digital-rights reviewers. Commercial demand is a hypothesis.

## Core workflow
Guided inputs: incident, jurisdiction, date, AI role, original instruction, permitted actions, actual action, actors and evidence descriptions.
Outputs: plain assessment; timeline; actor/control questions; candidate legal issues; evidence gaps; alternatives; source-status register; next review step.
Modes: plain language, law student and professional review.

## Demonstrations
Three labelled fictional examples: intentional AI-assisted fraud; an agent exceeding lawful instructions; third-party prompt injection. They are not judgments or measured product results.

## Integrity
No verified-law badge without direct verification. No numeric confidence/guilt scores. No fabricated citations. If source retrieval is unavailable, show that limitation before conclusions. Preserve the original ten-case pilot as dated background, not AI-specific holdings.

## Human control
Maintain authenticated owner review and a record of approvals. Authorisation must be checked on the server. Approval records do not constitute legal validation or perform external actions. A production evidence store requires defined access, retention, deletion and custody controls.

## Platform responsibilities
GitHub: public methodology, source register, fictional scenarios, historical pilot and changelog.
Sites Alpha: existing private working prototype and approval interface.
Replit: separate research demonstrator; provenance links are not synchronisation.
Base44: update an existing verified Legal Lens app if found; do not repurpose unrelated apps.
Drive: working research materials and a separate dated change folder.
Library: retained research files and change pack.

## Acceptance checks
User can describe an incident and receive a bounded assessment; a supplied official URL cannot inflate evidence status; out-of-scope input is labelled; fictional material cannot appear as case law; lawful authorisation and account compromise remain alternatives; the owner approval route remains server-controlled; existing data and licensing are preserved.

## Not yet established
Live legal-source retrieval, legally verified outputs, forensic log ingestion, automatic cross-platform synchronisation, commercial readiness and academic approval. Describe actual implementation separately from this specification.


## Citation verification update — 9 September 2026
Every legal claim must pass authority existence, passage support and jurisdictional/factual applicability checks. Current legal status, adverse authority, provenance and named review are also required. A supplied URL is a lead, not verification. Failed, missing, stale or conflicting checks block verified labels and final legal approval. Corrections invalidate dependent checks and approvals. Administrative permission is not legal validation.

The controlling standard is [Citation Verification Standard](https://github.com/moses-oroni/legal-lens-kenya/blob/main/docs/methodology/Citation_Verification_Standard.md). The Alpha remains a guided checklist without live source verification; implementation status is recorded separately.
