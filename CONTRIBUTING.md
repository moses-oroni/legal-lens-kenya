# Contributing

Contributions are welcome where they improve legal accuracy, appellate-status tracking, accessibility, reproducibility, or the clarity of the framework.

## Before submitting

1. Link legal claims to a primary source, preferably Kenya Law for Kenyan judgments and legislation.
2. State the date on which a changeable proposition was checked.
3. Distinguish the court's holding from a party's argument and from your own interpretation.
4. Explain any change to a case's appellate or implementation status.
5. Do not submit personal, confidential, sensitive, or restricted data.
6. Disclose material AI assistance in the pull-request description.

## Suggested issue labels

- `source-correction`
- `appellate-status`
- `methodology`
- `accessibility`
- `documentation`
- `privacy`

By contributing, you agree that your contribution may be distributed under the repository's applicable licences.
